Six guarantees, all enforced in code — not in prompts.

  • Nothing runs without your “evet”.
  • The card is rendered from verified data, not from the model’s words.
  • Every ID must come from a real lookup in the same run.
  • Business limits are enforced twice: by the agent and by the KEDD server.
  • Every write is idempotent and audited.
  • No customer phone number ever reaches the model or the UI.
